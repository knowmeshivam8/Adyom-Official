# Adyom Foundation - Program Requirements & Structure

This document outlines the structured requirements, program details, and technical features for the Adyom Foundation platform, corrected and mapped directly from the program matrices.

---

## 1. Drishti
- **Duration**: 1 Day.
- **Focus**: Employee Wellness program. It acts as the first step of "Art with Awakening" or mindfulness.
- **Target Audience**: Exclusive workshops for Corporates, Small-Scale Businesses, and Schools.
- **Formats**: Individual level (webinar), corporate workshops, and school workshops.
- **Registration & Access**:
  - Users can register as an individual or as a group for personalized workshops.
  - **Community Meets**: No separate registration is required. However, details must be accepted while accessing the meeting to keep track of learners and send reports to organizations. (If a company doesn’t allow employee details to be accessed, they can skip it; if they allow, the same individual registration form is used).

---

## 2. Chaitanya & Sparsh
- **Focus**: Both programs follow the exact same structure, separated only by age difference.
- **Duration**: 1 Year (49 weeks).
- **Target Audience**: Learners can buy directly or through a webinar.
- **Content Structure**: 
  - Works on 7 or more types of tribal and Folk Art (modules).
  - Learners get pre-recorded videos of fundamentals.
  - Every Art form has pre-recorded videos and a downloadable Material List. 
  - Each module consists of **4 sessions of creating artwork** and **2 sessions applying it on a usable surface** (e.g., painting on coasters, fridge magnets).
  - Recorded sessions of weekly live classes must be listed under the currently running Art module.
- **Module Admin Control**: 
  - The site may have 10 modules available, but the program will support 7 of them. 
  - Admin decides which 7 modules are active and is free to change them. Locking and unlocking modules is Admin-specific.
- **Access & Security**: 
  - Learners can access all videos of a particular Indian Art going on when they join, irrespective of their join date.
  - One user can log in only from one system at a time.
  - Users cannot record any video at any point.
- **Assignments & Submissions**:
  - After every module, students must submit at least **one artwork** and **one Activity work**.
  - Learners can share their work; once approved by the admin, it displays as testimonials or in a gallery.
- **Certification & Awards**:
  - **Excellence Certificate**: Provided after submitting 3 module assignments and 6 module assignments.
  - **Awards**: Max Attendee or Max Submission award, Most Innovative award (if tracking is enabled).
  - **Completion Certificate**: Provided after the complete program.

---

## 3. KalaPath
- **Duration**: 6 Months (Online) or Flexible (Offline).
  - Also features a 2-day Workshop (offline or flexible duration offline).
- **Format**: Similar to Coursera. Learners select the Artform or Art modules from the pre-recorded sessions.
- **Submissions**: Assignment work will be submitted similarly to Chaitanya. The program will ask for the "Module Name" while submitting the artwork.
- **Completion & Certification**:
  - All program videos can be accessed online. 
  - After completion of the videos, learners can apply for a completion certificate. 
  - In special cases (Offline Training), they get the certificate after submitting 5 Artworks.
- **Sponsorships**: Need to explore if the KalaPath program (or others) can be sponsored by companies so the amount paid is sponsored or free.

---

## 4. KalaVritti
- **Duration**: 6 Months (Online) or Flexible (Offline).
- **Focus**: Applied or Commercial Art. The core focus is to train underprivileged Girls and give them a platform to sell their products (both online & offline). 
  - These kids will learn offline, but their products will be sold by Adyom online.
- **Program Details**: 
  - Features a Skill building Program - offline (only for groups, not individuals) or Online (6 months duration).
  - Fixed modules: **Fundamentals & Business**.
  - An activity-wise material list is a downloadable common feature in all modules.
  - Technically similar to buying courses. The rest of the structure is the same as KalaPath.

---

## 5. Pratibimb
- **Duration**: 41 Days (5 Sessions).
- **Focus**: Not just an art course; it is a 41-day creative sadhana where art becomes a mirror, the canvas becomes a journey, and daily practice helps participants see their inner direction more clearly.
- **Access**: Direct Buy available (along with KalaPath & KalaVritti). Basically structured similarly to KalaPath and KalaVritti.
- **Dashboard Requirements**:
  - Create a Pratibimb Login Dashboard.
  - Learner registration and login.
  - Program access after enrollment.
  - Video structure: **3 days videos, 2 day live class, 41 days warmup videos** (may be the same video repeated weekly, 6 unique videos for 41 days).
  - Daily attendance marking.
  - Canvas progress photo upload (optional).
  - Admin dashboard to view attendance, progress, and provide live session links.

---

## 6. Registration for Underprivileged Learners
- **Submission**: Use the same Chaitanya form for submitting their work to get a Completion certificate.
- **Certificates**: Admin can generate Certificates directly to these students.
